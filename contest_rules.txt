Winner Selection Method:  
Round 1: All Entries will be evaluated against that month's project requirements using the following 
weighted criteria: 
○​
Execution (25%)​
 1: Does not meet the project requirements or does not function reliably​
 2: Meets some project requirements; major gaps or inconsistencies​
 3: Meets all project requirements; core functionality works​
 4: Meets all project requirements and works well with minor issues; mostly 
complete​
 5: Exceeds the project requirements; fully functional, stable, and demo-ready 
end-to-end 
○​
Creativity (25%)​
 1: Common idea with minimal differentiation​
 2: Slight variation on a common idea​
 3: Interesting twist or thoughtful angle​
 4: Distinctive idea with notable originality​
 5: Fresh concept, unexpected use case, or particularly clever implementation 
○​
Usefulness / Value (25%)​
 1: Hard to see the point or appeal​
 2: Limited or niche appeal; impact is unclear​
 3: Clear practical or experiential value for its intended use​
 4: Strong value with clear benefit to its intended users​
 5: Highly compelling — meaningfully serves its purpose or delights its intended 
audience 
○​
Polish & Thoughtfulness (25%)​
 1: Rough, confusing, limited usability​
 2: Basic usability but lacks refinement or clarity​
 3: Usable with basic UX considerations​
 4: Well-polished with thoughtful design and usability​

 5: Feels intentional and "real," with thoughtful details, edge cases, and clear 
guidance 
Round 2: The top 20 Entries from Round 1 will be evaluated on the same criteria listed above by a 
panel of judges from Handshake or partner sponsors.
